  the page model is not a new assumption, it is the model that already
  explains a banked counter to within a page.

== 6. WHAT THE CENSUS DECIDES ==
  1. Slices are NOT page multiples on either model (section tables), so
     no approach can promise byte-exact commit. Every approach carries
     an edge term, and the prereg names it rather than rounding it away.
  2. The worst-case VMA count under an mprotect commit is small against
     this box's vm.max_map_count, by three orders of magnitude, so
     approach A is not blocked by map-count exhaustion. That was the
     one cheap way approach A could have died and it does not.
  3. The edge term is bounded and tiny (fractions of a percent of the
     model), so it cannot rescue or ruin the P2 bar either way. It is
     carried explicitly in the bar so a surprise there is visible.
