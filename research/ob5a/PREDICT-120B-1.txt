== 0. WHY THE RS053 120b ROUTE LOG IS NOT THE PREDICTION SOURCE ==
  Measured, not argued. The RS053 run's own command line, verbatim from
  /mnt/f/f32/stage/research/rs053/runs/120b-prose-a/stderr.txt:
    -f /mnt/f/f32/stage/research/rs053/corpus-prose.txt --ctx-size 4096
    --chunks 4 -b 4096 -ub 4096 --threads 10 -ngl 99 -ncmoe 36
  This leg's frozen 120b configuration, verbatim from
  /root/ob1b/runs/pag120-prose-a/stderr.txt:
    -f /mnt/f/f32/stage/research/ob1/AC-PROSE.txt --ctx-size 1024
    --chunks 8 -b 1024 -ub 1024 --threads 8 -ngl 0 --no-repack
  Three differences, each of which alone breaks the prediction:
    corpus        rs053/corpus-prose.txt  vs  ob1/AC-PROSE.txt
    micro-batch   4096 tokens             vs  1024 tokens
    token budget  16384 (4 x 4096)        vs  8192 (8 x 1024)
  lease_events and peak_concurrent are both defined per MICRO-BATCH, so a
  4096-token chunking cannot predict a 1024-token run even on the same
  corpus. The RS053 log's role is unchanged and unmixed: it is the
  RANKING corpus that produced RESIDENT-SETS-120B-K8.json, and step 2
  verifies exactly that.
