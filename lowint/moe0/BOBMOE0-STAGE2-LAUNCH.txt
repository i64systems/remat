7. THE RESUME STORY (plan s.5, restated)
========================================
The funded run is a SEGMENT CHAIN, not a single unbroken process, and that costs
the determinism claim nothing. Every checkpoint banks a digest and a full sidecar
carrying the optimizer state, both RNG states and the step index, so any segment
can be restarted exactly where the last one stopped; G1-CARD limb (ii) proved at
a real boundary that a resumed segment reproduces the straight run's checkpoint
bytes exactly, and the sidecar field comparison showed the whole optimizer state
and both RNG states matching too. Therefore a run that is preempted by her demo
file, stopped by the temperature rule, or crashed, and then resumed from its last
banked checkpoint, carries the SAME whole-run determinism claim as an
uninterrupted one: the final bytes are a pure function of code, data pin and
seeds, independent of the preemption pattern. This launch has already
demonstrated it live, not just inherited it: the funded moe segment's metrics
over steps 1001..1100 are byte-identical
(6ad034e40b1900164d1e5477994d8732f13f1289fe4ed65ce11d6e8e420febd1) to G1-CARD
runA's over the same steps, computed in a different process from the same anchor.
If a sidecar is ever missing or corrupt from a crash mid-write, resume falls back
to the previous complete checkpoint pair; if full state restoration is ever
impossible, the claim NARROWS honestly to per-segment determinism and the receipt
says so in those words. A resume launch reuses the same launcher with SEG=seg02
and RESUME_MOE / RESUME_DENSE pointed at the last good checkpoint, which keeps
this segment's logs intact beside the next one's.
