9. LESSONS-FOR-BIG-WEIGHTS (the lane's standing coequal bar)
============================================================
L1. DO NOT BUDGET COMPRESSION ON ORDER-0 TERNARY SKEW. At convergence
    our trits sit within 0.0005 bits/trit of log2(3): symbol
    frequencies carry nothing. The BitNet-2B pin's 1.560314 came from
    somewhere our recipe/scale does not reach (scale, recipe, or their
    training data). Compression of big ternary weights must come from
    HIGHER-ORDER structure: context modeling (the LI-S12 order-2
    generator container), cross-tensor structure, or remat (store the
    generator, not the bytes).
L2. THE CALLED-NEVER-LOADED SHAPE SURVIVES ON QUALITY, DIES ON NATURAL
    SKEW, at this scale: equal-active MoE matches dense, so 8x
    capacity-by-call is free on the quality axis. But popularity-
    ranked residency (N4/A1) gains nothing when routing is near
    uniform: 4-of-8 residency covers only 55.2 pct of traffic, barely
    above the 50 pct of uniform. For the 1T horizon: either the
    prefetcher LEARNS the schedule from exact replay journals (RS050:
    lookup, not prediction; unaffected by this kill) or skew must be
    ENGINEERED (weaker/other balancing, specialization pressure),
    which is a new prereg, not an amendment.
L3. ROUTING DRIFTS TOWARD UNIFORM OVER TRAINING under Switch
    balancing (P_half 0.584 -> 0.552, H 0.9875 -> 0.9950). Early-run
    routing overstates final skew; any residency policy tuned on
    early checkpoints would be wrong at convergence. Prefetch/lease
    designs must track drift or train on final-regime traces.
L4. THE DETERMINISM ENVELOPE SCALES. bf16 autocast on the card, a
    preempted-and-resumed segment chain across an epoch-wrap bug fix,
    and every measurement byte-identical twice, at 163.8M params and
    120M selection slots. The single-run-statistic objection to the
    field's numbers (SYNTHESIS-1 s.4) does not apply to ours: the
    moat instrument works at funded scale.
L5. ZERO DEAD EXPERTS EVERYWHERE, from step 2000 to the end. Switch
    alpha 0.01 fully prevents collapse at this scale; if skew is ever
    engineered on purpose, alpha is the first dial, and collapse must
    be re-watched because this run proves nothing about weaker alpha.
