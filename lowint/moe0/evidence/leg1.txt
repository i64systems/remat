4. THE TRAINER
==============
One codebase, config-driven for arm and stage:
  lowint/moe0/train/bobmoe0.py         model, data, quantization, train loop
  lowint/moe0/train/compress_curve.py  the compressibility instrument
  lowint/moe0/train/check_arch.py      parameter-arithmetic check
  lowint/moe0/train/run_stage1.sh      the driver
  lowint/moe0/train/assemble_g1.sh     the G1 assembly

Implemented exactly to prereg s.1: BitNet b1.58-class ternary QAT, absmean
weight law W_t = round(clip(W/gamma, -1, +1)) with gamma = mean(|W|) and STE on
backward; 8-bit absmax per-token activation quantization; RMSNorm; SwiGLU FFN
(gate, up, down); rotary positions; TIED embedding/output head; byte vocabulary
of 259 symbols (256 bytes + BOS + EOS + PAD), identity tokenizer, no merge
tables. Norm weights, embeddings and the router stay float; all four attention
projections and all FFN matrices are ternary. No biases anywhere (the prereg
parameter arithmetic carries no bias terms).

ROUTER, as frozen: linear d_model x E, top-k=2, deterministic, no noise, no
jitter, no stochastic dispatch. Tie-break to the LOWER expert index, implemented
as a stable sort: torch.argsort(-logits, dim=-1, stable=True), then the first k
columns. Softmax over the selected k logits weights the expert outputs. Switch
auxiliary load-balance loss with alpha = 0.01.

Expert dispatch is a fixed-order loop over experts using index_select and an
out-of-place index_add with unique indices, so the accumulation order is fixed
by construction rather than by any runtime scheduling decision.

8. DEVIATIONS AND RESOLVED AMBIGUITIES, NAMED
=============================================
NO DEVIATION FROM THE PREREG'S FROZEN CONTENT. Nothing renegotiable was
renegotiated: the arms, the quantization law, the router form and tie-break, the
recipe, the seeds, the split rule, the checkpoint steps and the gates are as
committed. Data used the PRIMARY, so the fallback was not invoked. G1 passed at
8 threads, so the drop-to-1 fallback was not invoked. No leg needed the card, so
there is no stage-2 refusal to bank.

The prereg is silent on four implementation points. Each is named here with the
choice made, so the choice is auditable and can be overruled before stage 2.

  D1. AUX-LOSS AGGREGATION ACROSS LAYERS. s.1 freezes the per-layer Switch form
      L_aux = alpha * E * sum_e f_e * P_e and alpha = 0.01, but not how the five
      MoE layers combine. Implemented as the MEAN over MoE layers, so alpha keeps
      its per-layer meaning. Also, f_e is normalized by N*k so that sum_e f_e = 1
      (the Switch normalization generalized to top-2), and P_e is the mean of the
      full softmax router probability over tokens. Gradient flows through P only,
      as in Switch.
