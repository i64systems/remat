==========================================================
1. BIT-REPRODUCIBLE QAT IS FREE AT MULTI-THREAD ON CPU. Both ternary
   arms, including the MoE with its router, hit byte-identical
   checkpoints at 8 threads across processes under different load. The
   determinism envelope that train-1 proved for dense float training
   transfers to ternary QAT and to MoE routing without a single
   thread-count concession. For the big weights: the stage-2 CUDA
   envelope is the only remaining unknown, and it starts from a proven
   CPU reference to diff against.
2. TABLE COST DOMINATES CONTEXT GAIN AT EXPERT GRANULARITY. On a 32 KB
   expert block, order-0 coding WINS (0.795868) and order-2 LOSES
   (0.801514): context modelling bought 7 bytes and its tables cost 192.
   LI-S12 showed order-2 winning on a 17.4 MB layer by 0.03 percent.
   Consequence for the N4 called-never-loaded brain: per-region
   generators must size blocks against table cost or pool context
   models across blocks; per-expert order-2 is a net loss. The lease
   unit and the coding unit are different design axes.
3. COMPRESSIBILITY IS A TRAINING OBSERVABLE, NOT A FORMAT PROPERTY. At
   500 steps both arms sit at 1.584 bits/trit - init-dominated, less
   compressible than the trained BitNet-2B pin (1.560314). The
   instrument chain (quantize -> pack -> encode -> fresh decode ->
   byte-exact) now runs against our own checkpoints at every banked
   step, so stage 2 will show WHEN training starts leaving structure,
   per arm, per tensor class - the first curve of its kind on a native
   ternary MoE.
4. THE ROUTER'S SKEW IS EARNED, NOT GIVEN. An init-dominated top-2
   router under a Switch aux loss sits barely above uniform (P_half

   locality the whole thesis needs must EMERGE against the aux loss's
   uniform pull, which is exactly what makes the stage-2 P_half bar a
